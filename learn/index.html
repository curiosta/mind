How to learn

Judgment is the scarce skill.

Information is free and infinite. Learning is knowing which things are worth knowing, and which are worth doubting.

Two modes

Focused mode solves problems you already understand. Diffuse mode makes new connections. You cannot be in both at once. Twenty-five minutes, then a walk. Breakthroughs arrive in the shower because diffuse mode had clay to work with.

Chunking

A chunk is a concept compressed into one slot of working memory. Focus, understand, then know when to use it. Memorization without understanding falls apart when the context shifts.

Illusions of competence

Rereading feels like knowing. Close the book. Write what you remember. The gap is the learning.

The rule for AI

Use it to accelerate, never to replace. If you can't explain it without the tool, you borrowed it.

Barbara Oakley failed math in high school, then earned a doctorate in engineering. John Hutchinson's phrase: redirect the focus of learning.
