Defense

Not being fooled.

By a model or by a person. The useful stance isn't suspicion of everything. It's a habit of asking where something came from before you act on it. Decide on five messages first.

01 · Would you act?

Five messages. Go with your gut.

Do this first

Invented examples Names, numbers and links are made up. Don't overthink it. Answer the way you would on a busy day.

1 · Voice note, unknown number

"Bhai, it's Raju chacha. Stuck at the bank, my card's blocked. Send ₹15,000 to this UPI before they close at 4, I'll explain tonight."

Trap. Familiar voice, new number, a deadline, money. Step 3 catches it: call chacha on the number you already had.

2 · Class WhatsApp group

"Board maths paper LEAKED 🔥 join this channel, ₹499, tonight only, link expires at midnight."

Trap. The rush and the hope are the product. Steps 1 and 2 catch it: name the feeling, then ask who first said there's a leak. A forward isn't a source.

3 · SMS

"Dear customer, your electricity connection will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM as your last bill was not updated. Call officer at 98xxx xxxxx immediately."

Trap. A deadline, a threat, and a number supplied by the message. Step 3 catches it: use the number printed on your own bill or the provider's app.

4 · A video in your feed

A famous face, a familiar voice: "I use this app myself. It doubles your money in 30 days. Download from the link below."

Trap. Faces and voices can be made to order now. Steps 2 and 4 catch it: is it on the person's own channel? And separate the observation ("a video shows a face saying this") from the conclusion ("this person endorses it").

5 · A call from your sister's usual number

"Can you pay Anu's term fee? Use the school portal, the one you used last term. No rush, any time this week."

Fine to act. A number you already had, a portal you already use, no deadline, no code to share. It passes the checks in seconds. The point was never to suspect everything.

Your gut, scored

What to do, in order

  1. Don't act inside the feeling. Name it. That's Occlumency.
  2. Ask for the first source, not the forward. That's Priori Incantatem.
  3. If money, a password or a one-time code is in the message, stop. Call the person on a number you already had, not the one in the message.
  4. If it's a claim about the world, write the observation separately from the conclusion. If you can't, you don't have it yet.

02 · Two kinds of dark arts

The new kind

Text, a face, a voice, a short video: any of these can be made to order. A byline can be copied, and so can a familiar chat layout. So the question isn't "does this look real?" Real-looking is cheap now. The question is whether you can trace it.

A voice note that sounds like your uncle, asking for a transfer before the bank closes, has the same shape as an old trick. The voice is the new part. The hurry isn't.

The old kind

Repetition, fear, flattery, a deadline. All older than print: a forwarded "exam leak", a stock tip "from inside", a health cure "doctors won't tell you about". The machine didn't invent these. It made them personal, and it can make a thousand of them before lunch.

A daily minimum

So the drill is there when you're rushed: one specific memory in the morning, one repetition you noticed, and one thing you chose not to forward.
