Defense

Not being fooled.

By artificial intelligence or human intelligence. The new defense is not skepticism. It is verification.

The old dark arts

Repetition, fear, flattery, urgency. Older than writing. Faster now, and personalised. Same counter-spell: trace it back.

Daily practice
