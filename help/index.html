Get help now

If you need to talk to someone, start here.

mind is not a helpline. It cannot listen, advise or respond in a crisis. These are the numbers the organisations publish themselves, each checked on the date shown beside it (24 or 26 Oct 2026, IST).

In immediate danger? Call 112.

112 is India's single emergency number for police, fire and health emergencies, answered 24×7. Source: the Government of India's 112 India features page and FAQ. See "What we could not open" below for how we checked this line.

We don't tell you what you are feeling or what to do about it. The people on these lines are the ones to ask. This page only points to them, and shows where each number comes from.

01 · Government of India, free

The national line: Tele-MANAS.

  • Tele-MANAS

    14416 1-800 891 4416

    Toll-free, confidential, round the clock (24×7), across India. Two toll-free numbers, both shown on the Tele-MANAS site.

    Sources: Tele-MANAS site (both numbers, as written there) and PIB press release, 9 Oct 2026 (14416, round the clock) · checked 26 Oct 2026 (IST)

Correction, 26 Oct 2026. We removed KIRAN (1800-599-0019) from this page. A PIB release of 15 Feb 2024 reports that the KIRAN helpline was merged into Tele-MANAS, with KIRAN calls diverted to Tele-MANAS for three months and the first helpline then phased out (PIB, 15 Feb 2024, read 26 Oct 2026). A PIB explainer of 7 Feb 2025 also lists KIRAN as merged, but gives the year as 2022 (PIB, 7 Feb 2025). The two releases differ on the year, and we don't know the day the old number stopped connecting. We had listed it from a 2020 release and flagged it as not re-confirmed. If you have the old number saved, use Tele-MANAS instead.

02 · Non-government organisations

Other lines, each from its own site.

Hours are shown exactly as each organisation publishes them. Some are not round the clock.

  • Vandrevala Foundation

    +91 9999 666 555

    Free mental health counselling by call or WhatsApp. The site says 24×7, 365 days.

    The same site also sells paid sessions. That is separate from this free line.

    Source: vandrevalafoundation.com · checked 24 Oct 2026 (IST)

  • 1Life

    7893078930

    Suicide prevention and crisis support. The site says 24×7, by phone, email, video or chat.

    Source: 1life.org.in · checked 24 Oct 2026 (IST)

  • AASRA

    +91-22-27546669

    24×7 helpline for emotional crisis, mental illness and suicidal thoughts.

    Source: aasra.info · checked 24 Oct 2026 (IST)

  • iCALL (TISS)

    9152987821

    Free telephone and email counselling from the Tata Institute of Social Sciences. Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 9:00 pm. Not 24×7.

    Source: icallhelpline.org · checked 24 Oct 2026 (IST)

    9922004305 9922001122

    Distress helpline, all days, 10 am to 8 pm. The organisation describes it as a non-judgmental, non-advisory space and says it does not offer counselling. It is a listening line.

  • Sumaitri (Delhi)

    011-46018404 +91-9315767849

    Listening service, 12:30 PM to 5:00 PM, 7 days a week as published. Short hours, and they may change. Call within those hours.

    Source: sumaitri.net · checked 24 Oct 2026 (IST)

03 · If the trouble is money, not feelings

Been scammed online?

The Government of India's advice, in its press release on "digital arrest" scams, is to report straight away on the national cybercrime helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in. Source: PIB press release, 27 Oct 2024 · page read 26 Oct 2026 (IST).

This is the government's wording, not our advice. For how that scam works, and what we could and could not check about it, see the digital arrest case file.

04 · Left out, and why

What we could not confirm.

  • 112.gov.in, opened directly. The 112 site timed out on our direct fetches, so the 112 line rests on the pages' own text as returned by our search tool on 24 Oct 2026, not on a direct page read. The number is long-standing and widely published. Read the pages yourself if you can open them.
  • Tele-MANAS site, opened directly. On 26 Oct 2026 we read the Tele-MANAS site through a web reader. Our own command-line checker still can't make a secure connection to it, so that link may also fail for some browsers or networks.
  • The exact format of the second Tele-MANAS number. The Tele-MANAS site writes 1-800 891 4416. A PIB release of 10 Oct 2022 writes it 1-800-91-4416, and one of 7 Feb 2025 writes 1800-89-14416. We list the Tele-MANAS site's format, whose digits match the 2025 release. If it doesn't connect, use 14416.
  • Sneha, Roshni and hospital-run helplines (for example Fortis): we have no confirmation of their current numbers and hours from an official page, and our earlier research found some hours out of date or only a directory listing. Not listed.
  • iCALL: the page also shows an older Mumbai landline and two email domains in places. We list only the mobile number and the hours stated in the service's own description.
  • Peer-support groups. Group schedules change often and aren't helplines. They live on the calendar, as pointers.

05 · Keeping this page honest

Checked 24 Oct 2026, corrected 26 Oct 2026. Re-check due by 23 Nov 2026.

Helpline hours and numbers change. We re-check every line on this page against the organisation's own page at least every 30 days, and show the date next to each line. If a line can't be read, it is removed or marked unconfirmed, not left as it was. More on the rules is in how we check.

Spotted a wrong number or hours? Say so in the comments on the About page (needs a GitHub account). Please don't post personal details there. If it is urgent for you, call a number above instead.
