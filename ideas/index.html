The ideas

Five definitions. Everything else hangs off these.

This page is the spine of the site. The spells are the practice. The learning page and the defense page are the application. But the ideas underneath are what the site actually believes.

01 · One skill, two directions

Learning and defense are the same skill.

The Patronus and Priori Incantatem are the same muscle. Building a memory you can actually recall, and tracing a claim back to its source, both require knowing what you experienced rather than what was presented to you. The person who cannot recall what they saw cannot tell it apart from what was written for them. The person who cannot trace a claim cannot tell it apart from what was manufactured. One skill, pointed inward and outward.

02 · The barrier moved

Information used to be scarce. Judgment is scarce now.

When information was hard to reach, access was the product. That is what search engines sold: a way to find what existed. Information is now free and abundant, which means the scarce thing is no longer finding it. It is deciding what is worth knowing, and proving to yourself that you know it when the tool is closed. The site's job is not to deliver information. It is to build the judgment that makes information usable.

03 · The dark arts are real

They were just renamed.

Urgency, flattery, repetition, a familiar voice. These are old tricks, older than print, and the machine did not invent them. It made them cheap to produce at scale. A voice note that sounds like your uncle asking for a transfer before the bank closes is the same shape as a scam from a hundred years ago. The voice is the new part. The hurry is not. The spells are the counter-moves, and each one is a learning skill in disguise.

04 · Close the tool

A model is a tutor only if you close it and write.

If you cannot rebuild the argument without the screen, you borrowed the explanation. You did not learn it. The bill comes due the moment the tool is closed, and it always closes. Use the model to accelerate, never to replace. Ask it for a question, not an essay. Then answer with the tool closed. If you cannot explain the thing without it, return the explanation.

05 · Build last

Hutchinson's order: foundation, question, observation, deduction, model.

If the conclusion arrives before the evidence, you accepted it. You did not build it. Models are not proved. They are retired when an observation will not fit. The ability to state the model is not understanding. The ability to rebuild it from the foundation is.

The through-line

The scarce thing was never information.

It is the person who can hold a memory, trace a claim, and build a model, in that order. That is what this site is for.
