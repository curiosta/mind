The ideas

Five definitions. Everything else hangs off these.

This page is the spine of the site. The spells are the practice. The learning page and the defence page are the application. The ideas underneath are what the site actually believes.

01 · One skill, two directions

Learning and defence are the same skill.

The Patronus and Priori Incantatem are the same muscle. Building a memory you can actually recall, and tracing a claim back to its source, both require knowing what you experienced rather than what was presented to you. The person who cannot recall what they saw cannot tell it apart from what was written for them. The person who cannot trace a claim cannot tell it apart from what was manufactured. One skill, pointed inward and outward.

02 · The barrier moved

Information used to be scarce. Judgment is scarce now.

When information was hard to reach, access was the product. That is what search engines sold: a way to find what existed. Information is now free and abundant, so the scarce thing is no longer the document. It is the judgment that decides which document to trust, and whether you still have it when you look away. The site's job is not to deliver information. It is to build that judgment.

03 · The dark arts are real

Old tricks, newly cheap.

Urgency, flattery, repetition, a familiar voice. Older than print. What changed is the cost of aiming them: a model can write a fresh version for each reader, in that reader's words. The counter-move is still a learning skill. Name the pull, then trace the claim, then decide.

04 · Close the tool

Knowledge is what survives the closed tab.

The tool is allowed to speed the search. If the only copy of an idea sits in a chat log, you do not have the idea. You have a transcript. Transcripts do not come with you into a conversation, an exam, or a decision.

05 · Build last

Hutchinson's order: foundation, question, observation, deduction, model.

The order is the belief. Start from what a careful person could see, force a question out of that, and only then risk a model. A model that arrives early is someone else's conclusion worn as your own. You understand it when you can say which new observation would force you to give it up.

The through-line

The scarce thing was never information.

It is the person who can hold a memory, trace a claim, and build a model, in that order. That is what this site is for.
