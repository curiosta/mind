Why seven modules? One for each side of life where you can be pushed. A passing nod, not a source: the 2016 Telugu film Dhruva, whose director Surender Reddy says it borrows the age-old idea of Ashtadigbandhana, about trapping evil forces from every side (Times of India, 20 Dec 2016).
