How to learn, and how not to be fooled, in the world of AGI.

The dark arts are real. They're just not called that anymore. Every spell is a skill — and every skill has a counter-spell.

01 · The idea

Two things matter now.

How to learn when information is free and infinite. And how to tell what's real from what's manufactured — by machines or by people.

02 · The spells

Five spells. Each one is a real skill.

The Patronus

Strong memory as defense. If you can't recall what you actually experienced, you can't tell it from what was made up.

Occlumency

The pause between the feeling and the action. Name the emotion before you share.

Legilimency

Understand how people are fooled — including how you are fooled — before you try to defend against it.

Priori Incantatem

Trace a claim back to its source. A quote has a speaker. A statistic has a study.

The Foundation

Build from observation, not from conclusion. If the model arrives first, the spell has already failed.
